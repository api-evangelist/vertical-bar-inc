# Vertical Bar Inc.

> Vertical Bar Inc. builds CrossCheck, a DevOps and operations-visibility platform for NetSuite environments — configuration tracking, change management, deployment safety, record-and-replay regression testing, AI-assisted error analysis, and process mining — plus managed services and the FieldTrail browser extension.

## Product
- [CrossCheck platform](https://vertical.bar): NetSuite DevOps, visibility and change-management platform
- [Try CrossCheck Beta (free)](https://crosscheck.vertical.bar): sign in / free beta of the CrossCheck app
- [FieldTrail browser extension](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/fieldtrail-netsuite-field/ldhegbbcbdipnkacdnfkfkchldbknclg): NetSuite field-customization Chrome extension
